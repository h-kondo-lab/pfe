Of the 26 Standard Model parameters — three gauge couplings, $\lambda$, nine charged-fermion masses, four CKM parameters, $\theta_{\mathrm{QCD}}$, $v$, three neutrino masses and four PMNS parameters — the electron mass $m_e$ serves as the unit in which the dimensionless theory is displayed. Its specification is the setting of the absolute scale, not a fitted parameter, and $m_e$ itself is not counted as a prediction. Current deviations are due to the truncation order of the evaluations and can be improved with higher-order calculations.
